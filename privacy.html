Support Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

SARAS is a pregnancy companion app designed to help expectant mothers organize pregnancy information, prepare for prenatal checkups, and follow daily care plans.

This Privacy Policy explains the information SARAS handles, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, SARAS may process:

  • Pregnancy-related information
  • Daily symptoms and health notes
  • Questions for healthcare providers
  • Checkup schedules and preparation notes
  • Consultation summaries
  • Care plans and daily activities
  • Authorized Apple Health information
  • Voice input and consultation recaps

3. How We Use Information

SARAS processes information to support features such as:

  • Tracking symptoms and concerns
  • Preparing questions for doctor visits
  • Generating Checkup Briefs
  • Organizing consultation summaries
  • Creating and managing care plans
  • Displaying relevant health information
  • Supporting available voice interactions

4. Apple Health Integration

SARAS may request permission to access supported information from Apple Health.

Health information is used to provide additional context for supported pregnancy tracking and checkup preparation features.

You can review or revoke Health permissions through the Apple Health app or your device settings.

5. Voice Recap and Siri

SARAS provides voice-based features to help users capture consultation information and interact with supported app functions.

When you use Voice Recap, your spoken input may be processed to generate a consultation summary for your review.
