SARAS Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Support Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

SARAS is a pregnancy companion app designed to help expectant mothers organize pregnancy information, monitor daily health and well-being, prepare for prenatal checkups, record consultation takeaways, and follow daily care plans.

This Privacy Policy explains the information SARAS handles, how it is processed and stored, and the choices available to you. SARAS is built with a privacy-first, on-device architecture—we do not operate external servers to collect, store, or sell your personal or medical data.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you choose to use, SARAS may process the following information locally on your device:

  • Pregnancy Profile & Onboarding Data: Your name or nickname, age, height, pre-pregnancy weight, current weight history, and pregnancy dating information (last menstrual period date, ultrasound date and gestational age, or reported pregnancy week).
  • Checkup Schedules & Questions: Upcoming checkup dates, healthcare provider or doctor/midwife names, clinic locations, and questions you prepare for your visits.
  • Daily Check-Ins & Health Notes: Symptoms and their severity, mood and emotional feelings, vaginal bleeding logs, body temperature, blood glucose (including meal timing), water intake, blood pressure, and weight logs.
  • Consultation Notes & Care Plans: Medical checks performed during visits, test results, concerns discussed, doctor's advice, generated consultation summaries, and daily care plan tasks.
  • Authorized Apple Health Information: Health metrics and cycle records read from or written to Apple Health with your explicit permission.
  • Voice Input: Spoken answers provided when using the Voice Note feature to capture post-checkup consultation notes, or voice queries made through Siri.

No Account or Third-Party Tracking: SARAS does not require you to create an account, and it does not include any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or user-tracking tools.

3. How We Use Information

SARAS processes your information solely on your device to provide and improve your experience within the app, including:

  • Calculating gestational age, estimated due dates, and recommended weight gain ranges
  • Tracking daily symptoms, vitals, sleep patterns, activity levels, and emotional well-being
  • Detecting potential maternal health warning signs (such as elevated blood pressure) to prompt timely medical follow-up
  • Preparing and organizing questions and health snapshots ("Things to Consult") for prenatal visits
  • Transcribing, translating, extracting, and summarizing post-consultation notes into structured summaries and actionable care plans
  • Managing daily care plans, reminders, and Home Screen / Apple Watch widgets
  • Enabling voice shortcuts and consultation Q&A through Siri and Apple Spotlight

4. Apple Health Integration

SARAS integrates with Apple Health (HealthKit) to give you a unified view of your pregnancy health and to save measurements you log in one place. SARAS only accesses HealthKit data types that you explicitly authorize.

Data SARAS Reads from Apple Health

  • Body Measurements: Weight (Body Mass) and Height (used to calculate BMI and track pregnancy weight gain trends)
  • Vitals & Activity: Blood Pressure (Systolic and Diastolic), Resting Heart Rate, and Step Count
  • Sleep: Sleep Analysis (used to display recent sleep duration, stages, and bedtime patterns)
  • Reproductive Health (Cycle Tracking): Pregnancy records and Menstrual Flow (used during onboarding if you choose to import your last menstrual period date from Apple Health)

Data SARAS Writes to Apple Health

  • Pregnancy Status: Pregnancy category sample (start and estimated due date), recorded only when you confirm during onboarding
  • Measurements & Vitals: Weight (Body Mass), Blood Pressure (Systolic and Diastolic), Body Temperature, Blood Glucose (with meal timing), and Dietary Water
  • Symptoms & Bleeding: Pregnancy-related symptoms (such as nausea, vomiting, headache, dizziness, fatigue, palpitations, shortness of breath, memory lapse, lower back pain, abdominal cramps, heartburn, constipation, fever, pelvic pain, bloating, diarrhea, breast pain, generalized body ache, and fainting) as well as Bleeding During Pregnancy or Bleeding After Pregnancy
  • Mental Well-Being: State of Mind (momentary emotion valence and feeling labels logged during daily check-ins)

SARAS never uses Apple Health data for advertising, marketing, or data-mining purposes, and never transfers your HealthKit data to external third parties. You can review or revoke Apple Health permissions at any time in the Apple Health app or in your device's Settings > Apps > Health.

5. Voice Recap, On-Device AI, and Siri

SARAS offers voice-assisted features to help you capture post-checkup notes and interact with your saved pregnancy information hands-free.

  • No Audio Recordings Retained: When you use the Voice Note feature, SARAS listens to your microphone in real time to turn your spoken words into text as you speak. Raw audio is processed in memory only and is never recorded or saved as an audio file on your device or elsewhere.
  • Speech Recognition & Translation: Spoken input is transcribed using Apple's Speech Recognition framework (using on-device recognition whenever supported by your device, or Apple's speech recognition service when on-device recognition is unavailable) and translated locally when needed using Apple's Natural Language and Translation frameworks.
  • On-Device AI Processing: SARAS uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models (Apple Intelligence) to extract medical checks, concerns, and doctor's advice from your notes, generate consultation summaries, and answer questions about your past consultations. All AI extraction and summarization runs locally on your device.
  • Siri & App Intents: You can use Siri to add or update care plan tasks, manage questions for your doctor, hear your latest consultation summary, or ask questions about saved consultations.

6. Data Storage and Sharing

Data Storage

All data you enter into SARAS is stored locally on your device:

  • Local App Database: Consultation notes, summaries, transcript chunks, care plan tasks, and doctor questions are stored on-device using Apple's SwiftData framework.
  • Device Preferences: Your pregnancy profile and checkup schedule are stored locally in on-device storage (UserDefaults).
  • Apple Health: Health metrics and symptoms you choose to log are stored in your device's encrypted Apple Health store.
  • On-Device Search Index: To support Siri and local search, SARAS indexes your tasks, questions, and consultation notes on-device using Apple's CoreSpotlight and App Intents frameworks.

Third-Party Services

SARAS does not use any third-party cloud databases, analytics providers, crash-reporting services, or external AI APIs. All system services used by SARAS (HealthKit, SwiftData, Speech, Translation, Foundation Models, App Intents, WidgetKit, and CoreSpotlight) are native Apple frameworks governed by Apple's operating system privacy protections.

Data Sharing

We do not collect, access, sell, rent, or share your personal or medical information with any third parties. Your data remains on your device and under your control.

7. Data Security

We recognize the high sensitivity of personal and pregnancy-related health information and rely on Apple's built-in hardware and operating system security protections to safeguard your data:

  • All app data stays within the iOS application sandbox on your device.
  • Medical consultation search indexes in CoreSpotlight are configured with complete file protection (NSFileProtectionComplete), ensuring the index is encrypted and inaccessible whenever your device is locked.
  • HealthKit data is encrypted on your device by iOS when your device is locked with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.

While we design SARAS to keep your information exclusively on your device, no method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security. We recommend keeping your device protected with a strong passcode and up-to-date operating system software.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You have full control over the permissions granted to SARAS. You can manage or revoke access to Apple Health, the Microphone, Speech Recognition, and Siri & Search at any time through your device's Settings app.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because your information is stored locally on your device, it is retained only for as long as you keep it:

  • Within SARAS: You can edit or delete individual questions, care plan tasks, and saved consultation notes directly inside the app (which also removes them from the on-device Spotlight and Siri index).
  • Uninstalling the App: Deleting SARAS from your device permanently removes your local database, pregnancy profile, and on-device search indexes stored by the app.
  • Apple Health Data: Data previously written to Apple Health remains in the Apple Health app even if you uninstall SARAS, so you do not lose your health history. You can view or delete any entries created by SARAS directly within the Apple Health app at any time.

9. Medical Disclaimer

SARAS is intended to support pregnancy organization, checkup preparation, and personal care management.

SARAS does not provide medical diagnoses, prescribe treatments, or replace professional medical advice. Health alerts and AI-generated consultation summaries are for informational and organizational purposes only and should always be reviewed for accuracy.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding any medical questions, symptoms, or pregnancy concerns.
