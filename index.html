About SARAS

SARAS is a pregnancy companion app that helps expectant mothers track their symptoms, prepare for prenatal checkups, organize consultation summaries, and follow daily care plans.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I record my symptoms?

Open the Today tab and select "Jot it down" to record how you're feeling or save a question.

How do I prepare for a checkup?

Open the Checkup tab to review your saved concerns, questions, and Checkup Brief.

What is Voice Recap?

Voice Recap helps you capture what you remember from a consultation. You can review and confirm the summary before creating a care plan.

Does SARAS support Apple Health?

SARAS can use supported Apple Health information with your permission to provide additional health context.

Does SARAS provide medical advice?

No. SARAS helps organize pregnancy-related information and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
