About SARAS

SARAS is a pregnancy companion app organized around three main tabs to support you before, during, and between prenatal visits:

  • Today: Track your pregnancy week, upcoming checkup schedule, daily care plans, health metrics (weight, sleep, steps, and resting heart rate), and daily symptoms or questions.
  • Checkup: Prepare for your upcoming visit with your Things to Consult summary, and record your consultation takeaways afterward using text or live voice input.
  • Journey: Browse and search your complete history of past checkup records, consultation summaries, and doctor's advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I record my symptoms or questions for my doctor?

Open the Today tab, locate the "How is your body feeling today?" card, and tap + Add. Choose between logging a Question or a Symptom, then tap Add to Things to Consult. You can also manage your questions directly from the Checkup tab or by asking Siri.

How do I prepare for a checkup?

Open the Checkup tab and tap the Before Checkup card to open your Checkup Detail (Things to Consult). There you can review all your logged symptoms, saved questions, latest Apple Health metrics, and any maternal health follow-up alerts in one place.

How do I record Consultation Notes after my visit?

In the Checkup tab under After Checkup, find the Consultation Notes card and choose either Input Text or Record:

  • Record (Voice Note): Answer three guided prompts by voice—"What checks were done?", "What was the doctor's advice?", and "Anything else you want to add?". SARAS transcribes your speech live without saving any audio files, translates if needed, and uses on-device Apple Intelligence to pre-fill your checkup results, concerns, and care plan tasks for your review.
  • Input Text: Fill in the checks performed, test results, doctor's advice, and additional notes directly in the form.

Where can I find my past consultation summaries?

Open the Journey tab to view all your saved checkup records. You can search by keyword, pregnancy week, or note content, and sort your records to quickly revisit past results and advice.

How does SARAS work with Apple Health?

With your permission, SARAS reads your weight, height, sleep analysis, step count, resting heart rate, blood pressure, and cycle tracking data from Apple Health to display trends and pregnancy context. When you log your weight or consultation vitals (such as blood pressure or symptoms) in SARAS, they can also be saved back to Apple Health.

What if Apple Health, Microphone, or Speech permissions are turned off?

You can enable or manage permissions anytime in your device's Settings > Apps > saras, or manage Health data sharing directly in the Apple Health app under Profile > Apps > saras.

Can I use Siri with SARAS?

Yes. SARAS supports Siri and App Shortcuts so you can ask Siri to read your latest consultation summary, ask questions about your saved consultations (e.g., "Ask saras about my consultation"), or create and update care plan tasks and doctor questions hands-free.

Does SARAS provide medical advice?

No. SARAS is designed to help you organize pregnancy-related information, prepare for checkups, and follow your doctor's care plan. It does not provide medical diagnoses, prescribe treatments, or replace professional medical advice. Always consult your obstetrician, midwife, or qualified healthcare provider regarding any medical concerns.
